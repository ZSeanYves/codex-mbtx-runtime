Completion before efficiency

All assigned samples remain in the denominator. Step comparisons describe the subset where both arms completed successfully with complete step evidence.

Basic and complexity tracks

Each track keeps its assigned denominator, including failed and unstarted arms. The two repetitions belong to the same task; they are not independent tasks.

Actual decision steps by track

Both tracks include every captured outcome with a known total, including failures. Unknown totals remain separate from their observed prefixes; these distributions do not pool the two tracks.

Conditional paired estimates

Actual decision steps in the complexity track

All captured outcomes contribute when their total is known. Unknown totals retain their observed prefixes in the evidence. Design ranges are calibration targets, never required minimums.

Repeated attempts on the same task

Repeats are nested within fixed tasks. A difference records variation; it does not identify its cause. Failed attempts have no steps-to-success value.

Observed interaction costs

Reference pages, compiler and runtime failures, and external retries are separate observations. Activities may overlap in one decision; first successful compilation is not task completion or a repair-step count.

Execution evidence

Compiler starts, program launches and runtime denial messages are separate observations. Runtime denial messages do not provide a complete child-process audit; child-process totals remain unknown.
